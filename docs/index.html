Decoration kit

Every effect below uses only generated tokens. Nothing moves until you ask.

Rails and wash

--prism-identity-gradient, the surface-safe --prism-identity-wash, and the diagonal variant.

Conic ring

--prism-identity-conic keeps stripe proportions around a circle for avatars, progress rings and badges.

Glass over aurora

Aurora and glass

Per-stripe variables build a mesh; --prism-glass and --prism-glass-border float a readable panel on top.

Accent glow

--prism-glow is a two-layer shadow that lifts a focused or hovered control without changing its size.

Foil heading

A conic sweep of the accent and stripes clipped to display text. Decorative only; never body copy.

Chevron tab

Color with choice

Named, non-exhaustive palettes and a custom accent. Reference flag colors stay separate from readable interface colors.

Meaning stays intact

Errors, success, presence and progress retain their meaning. Games, projects and artwork are not tinted.
